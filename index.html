About the summit

Built in Vidarbha, for the world

The Vidarbh Innovation Summit brings the region's whole innovation ecosystem onto one platform. New startups meet more than 20 incubators, investors and panels in one place, something not available elsewhere in the region.

Students and startups bring their own ideas to solve real problems in Vidarbha, in any domain. The strongest solutions are shown at the summit, in front of the people who can fund, incubate or adopt them.

The summit is a joint initiative of aiKart and SGBAU Research and Incubation Foundation, Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University.

  • Students and innovators

    From colleges across Vidarbha and beyond.

  • Startups

    Early and growth-stage companies from every sector.

  • Investors and incubators

    20+ incubators and investors looking for the next growth story.

  • Government

    Departments looking for solutions they can adopt.

How it works

From application to the summit stage

  1. From 3 Nov

    Register and apply

    Pay the fee, choose your domain and upload your pitch deck.

  2. Ongoing

    Screening

    Entries are reviewed as they arrive. Results come with the shortlist.

  3. 2nd week of Dec

    Online round

    Shortlisted teams present to domain juries online.

  4. About 5 days later

    Physical round

    About 15 teams present in person in Amravati.

  5. Before Summit Day

    Grand finale

    Top teams are ranked overall and showcased at the summit.

Focus areas

Open to every domain

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Robotics
  • Defence Technology
  • Drones
  • Internet of Things
  • Agriculture
  • Healthcare

The theme is open. There are no fixed categories or problem statements: bring your own idea for a real Vidarbha problem in any domain. The focus areas above are only examples, and the top teams are ranked overall.

How entries are judged

  • You choose your domain in the form
  • Each entry goes to a jury from that field
  • Entries are judged on a working solution or pilot
  • Final results are an overall ranking across all domains

Who can apply

  • College students from any discipline
  • Startups up to 12 years old, with revenue under ₹100 crore
  • Teams from outside Vidarbha are welcome, as long as the solution addresses a Vidarbha problem

  • Your domain
  • A short description of the problem and your solution
  • Your pitch deck (upload)

FAQ

Hackathon questions

Are there separate categories or tracks?

No. The hackathon is open to every domain, and the top teams are ranked overall. You choose your domain in the form so that experts from your field judge your entry.

Can teams from outside Vidarbha apply?

Yes. Teams from Pune, Mumbai and other states are welcome, as long as their solution addresses a problem in Vidarbha.

Do we need a working prototype?

Entries are judged on a working solution or pilot, so the further along your solution is, the stronger your entry.

When will we hear if we are shortlisted?

Entries are screened as they arrive, and all teams are informed when the shortlist for the online round is announced.

Can school students take part?

Not in this hackathon. School students will be covered separately through a School Connect programme.
